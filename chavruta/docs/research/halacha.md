## Notes

1. **Sefaria covers the whole classical chain for OC 1–240.** Fetchable, public domain and linked from the SA seif (V-sef at 235:1 and 58:1): Tur, Magen Avraham, Taz, Machatzit HaShekel, Pri Megadim (both parts), Be'er Heitev, Sha'arei Teshuvah, Be'er HaGolah, Beur HaGra, Eliyah Rabbah, Mishnah Berurah, Biur Halacha, Kaf HaChayim, Chatam Sofer's glosses. Also linked: Levushei Serad, Ateret Zekenim, Chok Yaakov, Yad Ephraim, Dagul MeRevava, Eshel Avraham.
2. **Beit Yosef, Bach, Darkhei Moshe and Prisha hang on the Tur, not the SA.** The path is SA seif → `Tur, OC N:seif` (link type commentary) → `/api/links/Tur,_Orach_Chayim.N`.
3. **Rambam's commentaries hang on the MT halakha.** Kesef Mishneh, Lechem Mishneh, Mishneh LaMelech and Raavad are linked there. The MT↔SA/Tur links are untyped or "ein mishpat", e.g. MT Shema 1:9 ↔ SA 235:1, 235:3, Tur 235.
4. **Rif-line works hang on the Rif or Rosh.** Rabbeinu Yonah (→ Rif) and Ma'adanei Yom Tov / Divrei Chamudot (→ Rosh) are linked there, not to the Gemara. The Rif, Rosh and HaMaor themselves link to the Berakhot Gemara segments. The Ma'adanei Yom Tov anchors all collapse onto `Rosh on Berakhot 1:1:1`, so treat that data as broken.
5. **Some acharonim are not linked to the seif at all:** Aruch HaShulchan, Shulchan Aruch HaRav, Chayei Adam, Kitzur SA. AH and SAHaRav follow SA siman numbering (U), so a siman-level map (AH OC N ≈ SA OC N) is cheap. Chayei Adam and Kitzur need hand-made tables.
6. **Links are noisy.** Beyond the `commentary` type, the links API returns many untyped "Quoting Commentary" or citation links, e.g. MB 233:11 → SA 235:1. For "commentary on this seif", keep `type in {commentary, super_commentary, "super commentary"}` and `anchorRef == seif`.
7. **Missing from Sefaria (V-sef), with free text elsewhere:** Mordechai on Berakhot, Sha'ar HaTziyun, Birkei Yosef, Chazon Ish and Kaf HaChaim (duplicate) are on Hebrew Wikisource (CC BY-SA; MediaWiki `api.php?action=parse&page=…&prop=wikitext` should work, U, blocked here). Hagahot Maimoniyot is on AlHaTorah, which forbids reuse, so link only; Wikisource coverage is U. Pri Chadash and Pri Megadim are on beta.hebrewbooks.org `tursa.aspx` (text, no known API) and wiki.jewishbooks.org.il (MediaWiki, license U).
8. **Dicta library** (V-web, from the GitHub README fetch): CC BY-SA 4.0, with a `books.json` catalog giving `textFileURL` per book (`files.dicta.org.il/library-1-0/<name>/<name>__text_files.zip`, one text file per scanned page). Whether any title in this list is included is **U**: a summarized fetch of books.json found none, but that summary is not reliable.
9. **ToratEmet** (toratemetfreeware.com) is the source of several Sefaria texts, e.g. the Raavad (V-sef). Its own terms say redistribution is under CC 2.5 (V-web snippet); the exact variant is U.
10. **Modern works that should be link-only:** Piskei Teshuvot, Yalkut Yosef, Halacha Berura, Yabia Omer / Yechaveh Da'at, Chazon Ovadia, Or LeTzion, Shemesh uMagen, Mekor Chaim, SA HaMekutzar, Igrot Moshe, Shevet HaLevi, Minchat Shlomo. For Sefaria's CC-BY-NC works (Peninei Halakha, SA HaRav/Kehot), quoting is fine only for a non-commercial app with attribution.
11. **HebrewBooks URL patterns:** `https://hebrewbooks.org/<id>` (detail page) and `https://hebrewbooks.org/pdfpager.aspx?req=<id>&pgnum=<n>` (seen in Sefaria version metadata, V-sef). There's no text, so these can only be links.
